Muscle Mechanics

Hold each muscle’s key to contract it. Gravity never stops pulling, so choose the right muscles at the right time.

Level 1 · Bicep Curl

0.0s · 0.0s step

Elbow flexors: biceps brachii, brachialis, and brachioradialis

These muscles bend the elbow. The biceps also turns the palm up and assists shoulder flexion; the brachialis is a strong elbow flexor in any hand position. The triceps straightens the elbow. Keep the upper arm quiet and wrist neutral to control both lifting and lowering.

Movement checkpoints

  1. CONCENTRIC · Exhale and bend the elbows; keep elbows close to your sides, wrists straight, and shoulders relaxed. Pause at the top without swinging.
  2. ECCENTRIC · Inhale and lower smoothly until arms are nearly straight; keep control and do not let the weight pull your shoulders forward.
